OUR STORY.

We did not want to build another generic fast-food shop. We wanted a kitchen people would actually crave.

THE IDEA

BURGER.

Burger Kitchen began with a simple test: if you take away the fancy words, does the food still make you hungry? Our answer had to be yes.

We focus on strong fundamentals — properly seasoned patties, crisp vegetables, toasted buns, crunchy chicken, balanced sauces and food that arrives hot.
